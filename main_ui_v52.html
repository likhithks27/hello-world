🎓 Picking Method

Each session creates small assembly orders. The method changes how required parts are grouped into runs.

By OrderOne assembly order per run; simple and accurate.
Batch PickingGroups the same part across orders; faster but needs sorting.
KittingCollects parts into ready-to-use kits before assembly.
SequencingPicks parts in the exact line-consumption order.

▶ Levels of Digitalisation

This digital service has two parts: AMR navigation (this tool) and RFID pick verification (teammate's sensor, simulated here).

  • 🤖
    AMR auto: the robot guides you to each station; the pick button unlocks on arrival.
  • 🚶
    Manual: navigate using the warehouse map; the AMR is just a cart.
  • 📡
    RFID live feedback: each pick is scanned and confirmed correct or incorrect on screen.
  • 📡
    RFID no feedback: the scan is logged but the operator is not told the result.
  • ✋
    No RFID: you self-confirm picks with no scan.

📊 What to Compare

After paired runs complete, compare these metrics in Analytics:

  • ⏳
    Total time: how long the full pick sequence took end-to-end.
  • 📍
    Travel distance: how far the AMR moved between stations (from Live Monitor path data).
  • 🔄
    Decision load: in manual mode, how much time was spent finding the next station vs. actually picking.

🧪 Make a Prediction

Before creating runs, think through these questions and note your answers:

  • ❓
    Which picking method will produce the shortest total travel distance?
  • ❓
    Will AMR guidance reduce operator errors, time, or both? By how much?
  • ❓
    What happens to efficiency as picking capacity (batch size) increases?
  • ❓
    Which picking method will have the most waiting time at the assembly station?

Before the Run

  • Predict which method is fastest & why.
  • Estimate how much time AMR guidance saves vs. manual.
  • Predict whether RFID live feedback will cut pick errors.

During the Run

  • Where is time spent: moving, waiting, or deciding?
  • Does the AMR route look optimal to you?
  • Note which station takes the longest to reach.

After the Run

  • Compare AMR vs. manual times & distances.
  • Did the result match your prediction?
  • What would you change to improve efficiency?
